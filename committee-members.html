About The Committee

The Telonari Gram Panchayat Committee is a dedicated team of elected representatives and active members committed to the overall development and welfare of the village. The committee works together to ensure transparency, effective implementation of government schemes, proper management of community funds, and timely resolution of public issues. Through regular meetings and active participation, the committee strives to build a cleaner, stronger, and more prosperous village for every citizen.

Working together for Village Growth

Community Engagement

funds and expenses regularly.

Address public concerns and ensure welfare schemes reach citizens.

Meetings

Organize monthly meetings and record important decisions.
