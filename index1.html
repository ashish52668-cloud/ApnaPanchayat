WELCOME TO TELONARI PANCHAYAT

"Digital Panchayat, Majboot Gaon - Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas."

Telonari Gram Panchayat ek digital aur transparent platform
hai jo har nagrik ko Panchayat ki sevayon, vikas yojnaon aur
committee activities se jodne ka kaam karta hai. Yahan aap
committee fund manage kar sakte hain, voter ke roop mein registration
kar sakte hain, latest notices dekh sakte hain aur Panchayat
ke har mahatvapurn update se jude reh sakte hain. Hamara lakshya
hai ek smart, swachh, aur viksit Telonari ka nirmaan.

Telonari Gram Panchayat hamare gaon ke vikas, suvidha aur
samriddhi ke liye samarpit hai. Hamara uddeshya har nagrik ko
behtar sadak, swachhta, peene ka saaf paani, shiksha, swasthya
aur sarkari yojnaon ka labh pahunchana hai. Digital technology ke
madhyam se hum Panchayat ki sevayon ko aur adhik transparent,
efficient aur sabke liye aasaan banana chahte hain. Aaiye, milkar
ek Smart, Digital aur Atmanirbhar Telonari ka nirmaan karein.
