COMMITTEE RULES

The Telonari Panchayat Committee follows a set of rules and guidelines to ensure transparency, discipline, and effective village development. Every member is expected to work honestly, responsibly, and in the best interest of the community.

Rule 1 – Regular Attendance

Members should attend all committee and Gram Sabha meetings regularly.

Rule 2 – Respect Everyone

Treat every member and citizen with respect and maintain good behavior.

Rule 3 – Transparency

Maintain honesty and transparency in all committee activities and financial matters.

Rule 4 – Public Services

Work for the welfare and development of the village without discrimination.

Rule 5 – Active Participation

Take part in village development programs, awareness campaigns, and community events.

Rule 6 – Fund Responsibility

Ensure proper use and accurate records of committee funds.

Rule 7 – Follow Decisions

Respect and follow the decisions taken by the committee collectively.

Rule 8 – Misconduct

Any member involved in misconduct or misuse of authority may face disciplinary action.

Rule 9 – Save Public Property

Committee members must protect Panchayat property responsibly for the benefit of the entire village.

Member's Responsibilities

Attend meetings regularly. Support government schemes. Help resolve local issues. Maintain village cleanliness. Encourage citizen participation. Promote education and awareness.

Code Of Conduct

Be Honest
Be Responsible
Be Respectful
Work as a Team
Maintain Confidentiality
Follow Panchayat Rules
