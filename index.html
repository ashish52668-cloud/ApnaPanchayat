WELCOME TO TELONARI PANCHAYAT

"Digital Panchayat, Majboot Gaon - Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas."

Telonari Gram Panchayat ek digital aur transparent platform hai jo har nagrik ko Panchayat ki sevayon, vikas yojnaon aur committee activities se jodne ka kaam karta hai. Yahan aap committee fund manage kar sakte hain, voter ke roop mein registration kar sakte hain, latest notices dekh sakte hain aur Panchayat ke har mahatvapurn update se jude reh sakte hain. Hamara lakshya hai ek smart, swachh, aur viksit Telonari ka nirmaan.

Telonari Gram Panchayat hamare gaon ke vikas, suvidha aur samriddhi ke liye samarpit hai. Hamara uddeshya har nagrik ko behtar sadak, swachhta, peene ka saaf paani, shiksha, swasthya aur sarkari yojnaon ka labh pahunchana hai. Digital technology ke madhyam se hum Panchayat ki sevayon ko aur adhik transparent,efficient aur sabke liye aasaan banana chahte hain. Aaiye, milkar ek Smart, Digital aur Atmanirbhar Telonari ka nirmaan karein.

ABOUT GRAM PANCHAYAT

Building a Better Village Together

Gram Panchayat is the foundation of local self-government in rural India. It works to improve the quality of life of villagers by providing essential public services, promoting sustainable development, and ensuring transparency in governance. Through active participation of citizens, the Panchayat plans and implements projects that benefit the entire community. Our Digital Panchayat platform makes it easier for residents to stay connected, access important services, register as voters, join community committees, track monthly fund contributions, and receive the latest updates about village development.

Our Mission

To create a transparent, digitally connected, and self-reliant village by delivering efficient public services and encouraging active community participation.

Our Vision

To build a smart, clean, and prosperous village where every citizen has equal opportunities, access to essential services, and a voice in local development.

Sarpanch's Message
